1. Purpose of this document
This document brings together (a) the Global Projects company/service profile, (b) the project categories described in the company material, (c) the individual project briefs/SOWs currently available, and (d) the centre onboarding, operating and commercial framework. It is intended to be reviewed by management before the information is used in LEO or shared externally.
Review note: The document reports what the supplied source files state. It does not independently verify the legal status of entities, client identity, project availability, commercial promises or enforceability of contract clauses. Where source files disagree, the conflict is shown for management decision rather than silently choosing one value.
2. Company profile and positioning
The supplied company profile describes Global Projects as a US/UK-based outsourcing company with more than four years of industry experience. It presents the business as providing technology-driven outsourcing solutions to businesses and centres, and identifies the following service areas:
BPO and call-centre operations (inbound and outbound).
KPO and LPO services.
Backend support, including data entry, document preparation, record maintenance, virtual assistance and email support.
Lead generation for B2B and B2C through telecalling, email marketing and CRM tools.
Software and CRM-related projects, including web development, app development, CRM tools, billing software and custom solutions.
The profile states that Global Projects works in collaboration with Bitmax Group in India since 2024 and that support can include software/tools, technical setup, staff-hiring assistance, training, project setup and operational support. The profile also refers to eligible centres being able to receive direct client sign-up.
Management to confirm: Approve the exact legal identity, jurisdiction, licensing claims, group relationship, approved public description and the wording around “direct client sign-up”. The source profile uses several descriptions (US/UK-based, licensed in US/UK, Global Projects Associates and Bitmax Group collaboration); these should be reconciled against current corporate records and signed agreements.

11. LEO knowledge and response rules
LEO should be trained to answer Global Projects enquiries using the approved company profile and the relevant project-specific SOW. It must keep general company information separate from individual project details.
LEO may answer
What service categories Global Projects lists: BPO/call centre, backend, lead generation, software/CRM, KPO and LPO.
How inbound and outbound call-centre work differs.

Project-specific scope, country, language, shift, team structure, payout and infrastructure only when the relevant project document is approved for the caller’s access level.
The general onboarding process and centre-readiness requirements.
LEO must not do
Do not claim a project is currently available, guaranteed or allocated without a current status confirmation.
Do not mix Romania, France and Italy project details, payouts, staffing or KPIs.
Do not present presentation examples (BFSI, Europe chat, USA gaming) as active projects unless confirmed.
Do not invent client identity, project scope, payout, royalty, deposit, refund timing, penalty or go-live date.
Do not disclose internal-only commercial, legal, client-sensitive or security details to a public caller unless management explicitly approves that access.
Do not describe unverified licensing, registration, website or client claims as independently verified.
When information conflicts or is missing, state that an authorised representative must confirm the latest approved details.
Recommended response when asked about a specific project
